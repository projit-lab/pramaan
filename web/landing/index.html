Pramaan: the signature AI can't fake.

AI can fake a face, a voice, an invoice, even an approval click. Pramaan holds every risky company payment until the real approver signs that exact payment with a fingerprint, using a key sealed in their phone's secure chip. No signature, no payment.

Payment created→Held by policy→Approver signs on phone (StrongBox)→Cloud KMS countersigns mandate→Gate releases, only if nothing changed

Try it in 2 minutes (no sign-in)

  1. Create a payment in the ERP (New payment). Pramaan scores it and holds it.
  2. Watch it arrive on the console, marked "From the ERP". A bank file upload in the ERP arrives the same way, marked "From a bank file".
  3. Approval only happens on the approver's phone: a fingerprint over the exact payee, account and amount. The demo video shows it; the console already holds examples we approved on a Pixel 10a: one released, one tampered and refused, and a batch signed with one fingerprint.
  4. Verify a mandate yourself: open the verifier and paste MANDATE_ID. Your browser checks the Cloud KMS countersignature and the phone's signature, with no trust in us.
  5. Hack us: in the ERP admin panel, change the account number of an approved payment and press release. The gate refuses, because the payment no longer matches what was signed.

Why it matters

In 2024 a finance employee sent about $25 million after a video call where his CFO and colleagues were all deepfakes. Businesses have reported over $61 billion lost to business email compromise since 2013 (FBI IC3). Every one of those payments was approved by someone who believed it was real.

Built on Google Cloud: Cloud Run, Cloud KMS (HSM), Firestore, Firebase Hosting, Android Keystore with key attestation, Gemini. Roadmap: Play Integrity, AI payables agent, bank-side verification.
